What this experiment measures METHOD & SOURCES ↓

Every model receives consecutive frames decoded from the original 1920 × 1080 download. The video above is a high-quality H.264 preview. TrackNet-Pickleball and WASB use three-frame windows; TrackNetV3 uses eight frames plus a median background estimated from 16 frames in this excerpt. Windows are consecutive and non-overlapping; only the final window is padded. Raw 2D candidates have no gap filling or smoothing.

The three 3D views fit gravity and linear drag independently to each setup’s strongest candidate regions. They share the existing court calibration and manually reviewed opening bounce/contact events at 00:56.523–00:59.720. Bounce heights are constrained; contact heights are approximate priors. Fewer than five detections skip a flight. Outliers and implausible fits are rejected; unsupported gaps and times outside reviewed flights have no fitted height. A hollow marker maps the strongest 2D candidate through the court homography assuming ground level. It preserves detection visibility, but is not the ball’s true 3D location: height and horizontal position remain unknown. A candidate more than 18 image pixels from the fitted flight remains visible as a hollow marker beside the solid fitted ball. Fit RMS is reported in 1080p image pixels and does not establish accurate depth. The common player skeletons are the original YOLO pose estimates. These ball fits are regenerated from each setup’s 1080p predictions, not copied from the original viewer.

All three pretrained checkpoints were trained with 512 × 288 inputs. Larger inputs are experimental and can change the apparent ball scale. TrackNetV3’s 960 × 544 input has a small aspect-ratio change to satisfy its network dimensions. Candidate regions exceed a fixed heatmap threshold of 0.5. The strongest region is highlighted; secondary regions can be shown. Scores are model activations and are not comparable calibrated probabilities.

Coverage is not accuracy. A paddle mistaken for the ball still counts as a candidate. These excerpts have no complete reviewed ball labels. All 3D heights and paths are estimates. The original 3D reconstruction keeps its existing observations and reviewed flight fits.
