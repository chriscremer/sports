Game rules

  • Each match is one 24-minute game. Switch sides after 12 minutes.
  • If tied after 24 minutes, use rally scoring; the first team to 2 points wins. The team with the ball at the end of regulation serves first, with the player in position 1 serving.
  • Teams that lose can continue with friendly games; check the coordinator’s printed schedule for court assignments.
  • A substitute must have played with the team at least once in the regular season, or be of equal skill to the missing player.
  • For a disagreement: five seconds, no point awarded, and re-serve.

The league may adjust the schedule; check the original document before playoffs.
